A NOTE, IN A FEW TAPS

From a long answer
to your own little collection.

  1. Paste an answer in Edit mode.Paste Markdown or plain text, then switch to Select blocks. Switch back to Edit whenever you want to change the source.
  2. Tap the good parts.Each paragraph and list item is a block. Nested lists stay with their parent; tables and code stay whole.
  3. Review, then copy.Your picks keep their original order. Copy Markdown into Obsidian or download a .md file.

Your draft stays in this browser. Switching to Select blocks applies your edits and keeps picks for unchanged blocks. Clear source removes the answer and its selections; Undo clear restores them until you start a new answer.
